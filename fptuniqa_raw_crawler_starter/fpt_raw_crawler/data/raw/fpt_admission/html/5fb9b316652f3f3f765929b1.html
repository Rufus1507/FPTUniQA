Future forward
Global Ready

Twenty years ago, FPT nurtured the aspiration to build a distinctive university environment where students learn to work, to live, and to lead the future. From that aspiration, FPT University (FPTU) was born with the spirit of "real learning - real work - real success," pioneering the integration of businesses and practical projects into the classroom, opening a path for Vietnamese students to reach out to the world.

Over nearly two decades, FPTU has continuously transformed itself in line with the changing world, from a technology university to a global technology, entrepreneurship, and business university . Technology is the foundation for innovation, entrepreneurship is the mindset of self-reliance, and the global environment is an integrated learning space – all guided by Vietnamese identity: flexibility, resilience, and responsibility. Each FPTU student, upon entering the world, not only carries professional skills and a global mindset but also embodies Vietnamese resilience, ready to shape the future for themselves, their community, and their country.

Student life

Student life at FPT University is a dynamic learning journey, seamlessly blending knowledge, practical experience, and a multicultural environment. Students have the opportunity to study, interact, and gain experience across five campuses nationwide, while also participating in a diverse system of clubs, extracurricular activities, community involvement, and cultural and sports events. Furthermore, international exchange programs and overseas semesters help students broaden their global perspective, enhance their integration skills, and enrich their comprehensive academic experiences.

Campus

FPT University has five modern campuses: Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, Quy Nhon, and Can Tho – forming a nationwide learning network, opening up a journey of global connection. Students can rotate semesters between campuses, freely choosing their learning path, expanding their connections, and exploring different regions of Vietnam within their university experience.

Hanoi Campus

This is where the AI ​​Lab, Innovation Hub, and over 50 student clubs are located. From here, thousands of young technology, startup, and creative ideas have been born.

Ho Chi Minh City Campus

Located in the High-Tech Park, this learning environment connects you with over 1.000 businesses – turning your ideas into real projects.

Da Nang Campus

A modern, green campus in the heart of the coastal city, closely connected to the FPT business ecosystem and the Da Nang High-Tech Park.

Quy Nhon Campus

Where intelligence and technology intersect. Students have the opportunity to learn, research, and intern directly at FPT's leading AI center.

Can Tho Campus

A learning environment steeped in the unique character of the Mekong Delta, where students conquer technology and open doors to global integration.
